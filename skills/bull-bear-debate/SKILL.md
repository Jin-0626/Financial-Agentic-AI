# Bull Bear Debate

Use this skill when stress-testing an investment thesis, preparing a committee
debate matrix, or balancing catalysts against risks.

## Debate Rules

1. Build the bull case from supported catalysts, operating momentum,
   balance-sheet resilience, capital returns, and strategic optionality.
2. Build the bear case from supported margin pressure, leverage, cash
   conversion weakness, execution risk, liquidity risk, cyclicality, and
   disclosure gaps.
3. Challenge both sides. Do not list generic pros and cons.
4. Keep assumptions separate from facts. Unsupported assumptions belong in an
   explicit uncertainty list or evidence-limitation column.
5. Do not use current-quarter, YoY, or QoQ language unless the supplied evidence
   supports the period basis.

## Final Synthesis

- Prefer `HOLD` when the bull and bear cases are balanced or evidence is
  insufficient.
- Prefer stronger positive or negative ratings only when filing evidence and
  current market intelligence clearly support the asymmetry.
