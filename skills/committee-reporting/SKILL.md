# Committee Reporting

Use this skill when preparing the final user-facing Bursa equity report.

## Required Shape

1. Executive Summary & Core Identity
2. Segmental Revenue & Profitability Breakdown
3. Balance Sheet & Cash Flow Health
4. Bull vs. Bear Debate Matrix
5. Committee Verdict & Action Plan

## Writing Rules

- Use clear Markdown tables for segment, balance-sheet, debate, and verdict
  sections.
- Use `Unavailable in supplied evidence` for unsupported metrics.
- Use `Target range unavailable due to insufficient quantitative evidence`
  unless a source-supported valuation basis exists.
- Mention the evidence basis in plain language, such as latest quarterly filing
  excerpt or live market-intel summary, without printing raw URLs or chunk IDs.
- Keep the tone institutional, direct, and cautious about uncertainty.
